# Geographic companion to the knowledge graph

[Open the road map PNG](../output/geographic-preview/geographic-roads.png). The full graph's saved layout is a schematic: road positions were manually arranged and other topics used Graphviz. This companion view instead places all 10 physical location nodes at their recorded coordinates and draws the 9 road segments from the archived NJOGIS centerline features. Documents, rules, credentials and other nonspatial records remain in the full graph. No graph record or relationship is changed.

The geometry comes from the July 26, 2026 ArcGIS/NJOGIS snapshot already in the repository. Nine location coordinates are exact GIS endpoints. The gate comes from the July 31 Google Maps observation; Tripoli's line is clipped at its nearest centerline point, 0.1703 metres from that observed coordinate. Eight segment chains match both recorded endpoints exactly. Direction checks respect the archived GIS one-way attributes. The northbound ramps remain a candidate approach, not a verified truck route. Crossing background roads do not introduce new connections. The official ArcGIS layer metadata was reachable during this review, but current feature geometry and road conditions were not revalidated.

Rebuild with `python3 tools/author_geographic_preview.py`, then `rsvg-convert -o output/geographic-preview/geographic-roads.png output/geographic-preview/geographic-roads.svg`. The renderer uses the Python standard library and the existing rsvg-convert installation. The companion GeoJSON preserves the original IDs and attributes and can be loaded into QGIS or ArcGIS; `geometry-audit.json` records input hashes and the gate offset. The map uses a local equirectangular projection with equal metres per pixel and north up. Only annotation labels move for legibility. All 112 prototype tests passed after this addition, including rejection of disconnected GIS features, wrong-way traversal, and a gate far from its centerline.
