# Full graph with geographic roads — review draft

[Open the full-resolution PNG](knowledge-graph-geographic-draft.png). This is one complete graph image: 114 nodes and 281 relationships drawn from the archived 121 nodes and 345 relationships. The 10 physical locations use their recorded coordinates, and the 9 segment nodes sit halfway along the length of their archived NJOGIS centerlines. The APM gate moves into that geographic network, with its existing terminal relationships attached. Route and approach definitions (Route A/B, Turnpike SB/NB approaches) and the three road-evidence source dots are hidden from the picture — their segment order and citations stay in the graph data. Only geographic labels are offset to prevent overlaps; the underlying locations stay fixed. Heavy lines show GIS roads; thin arrows show graph relationships, including the direction of STARTS_AT and ENDS_AT relations rather than travel direction.

This draft uses the July 2026 archived geography already inspected for the geographic companion. The final Tripoli geometry is clipped beside the observed gate; the candidate northbound ramps remain marked as not truck verified. Source records and graph topology are unchanged. The current GitHub PNG has not been replaced or updated. Rebuild with `python3 tools/author_integrated_geographic_draft.py`, then render its SVG using the existing `rsvg-convert`. Full-graph labels remain small when fitted to a screen, and cross-topic relationships are still dense; this draft is for deciding whether the geographic integration belongs in the final PNG.
