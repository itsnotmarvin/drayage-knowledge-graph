    Welcome to the SUPERLOAD online permitting system. This site issues permits for oversize / overweight vehicles along State Routes Only.

    Port St. Oversize Load Restrictions
     
    Effective August 3, 2026, a section of Port St. will be reduced to one lane in both directions between Brewster Rd. and Marlin St. until further notice. In this section of Port St., lanes will be reduced to 11 feet wide and all oversize loads heading eastbound on Port St. MUST utilize the Corbin St. ramp. All oversize loads heading westbound on Port St. MUST follow the Marlin St. / Kellogg St. detour or utilize Outer Port St. until further notice. All oversize loads MUST exit through the south via North Ave. Please call the Port Authority Police Department (PAPD) at (973) 578-2180 to request an escort for any oversize loads travelling through the port.

    For any single permit request that utilize county/local route segments to connect between State routes, the permit request should identify the connecting routes(county/local) in comment area when sent for review. Requests without this information will be returned/denied.

    Permit requests for County, Local or Toll roads are handled through those respective agencies. To access contact information for County and Local authorities go to  http://nj.gov/nj/gov/county

    ROUTE SURVEY REQUIREMENTS:

    Route survey is required when the overall width exceeds 16' or height exceeds 14' 6''. Survey must be attached within your application.
